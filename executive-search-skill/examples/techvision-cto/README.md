## Role Requirements

**Title:** Chief Technology Officer  
**Reports to:** CEO  
**Team Size:** 150 engineers (projected growth to 250 in 18 months)

**Must-Haves:**
- Built/scaled engineering orgs 100-300 people
- Deep distributed systems & cloud infrastructure experience
- Led platform/infrastructure products at scale (millions of events/day)
- Experience with observability, monitoring, or developer tools preferred
- Strong architectural vision + hands-on technical depth

**Nice-to-Haves:**
- FAANG or FAANG-adjacent background
- Experience with time-series databases or streaming data
- Prior CTO role at $50M-200M ARR SaaS company
- PE or VC-backed company experience

**Company Culture Fit:**
- **Accelerator profile:** High-growth company needs urgency, visible leadership
- Comfortable with ambiguity and rapid change
- Technical credibility with senior engineers
- Strategic thinker who can also roll up sleeves
